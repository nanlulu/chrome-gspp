GSheet++

Row/column crosshair and cross-tab reference jump for Google Sheets

Crosshair

Off by default, so both bands share one color. Turn it on to tell the row and column apart at the intersection.

Bands blend with multiply, so text and your own cell fills stay readable underneath. Raise this if the tint is too faint on your monitor.

Reference jump

Click the button, then press the combination you want. Needs at least one modifier. Jumping back walks the full history, so you can follow a chain of references and retrace it.

Troubleshooting

Google changes the Sheets page structure from time to time. If the highlight or the links stop appearing, turn this on, reload a sheet, and check the console — or run __gspp.diagnose() there. Anything marked MISSING points at the selector that needs updating.

Feedback

GSheet++ collects nothing, so which settings you kept — and why you turned the others off — is the one thing it cannot work out on its own. The form is the whole feedback channel.
